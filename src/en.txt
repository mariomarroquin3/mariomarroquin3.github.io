### proyectos.010
Tail risk for Bitcoin and Ethereum

### proyectos.011
Which models best predict the tail risk of Bitcoin and Ethereum, and which ones fail?

### proyectos.012
<em>Out-of-sample</em> comparison of more than 20 volatility models that predict
              Value-at-Risk and Expected Shortfall one day ahead with a walk-forward protocol on a rolling 500-day window.

### proyectos.013
<b>Models.</b> GARCH-t, GJR, EGARCH, EWMA, FHS, GARCH-EVT, HAR-RV, Realized-GARCH, CAViaR, MS-GARCH, RF-QR, LSTM-Vol…

### proyectos.014
<b>Evaluation.</b> Kupiec, Christoffersen, Dynamic Quantile, Acerbi-Szekely, FZO, Diebold-Mariano, MCS and Berkowitz.

### proyectos.015
<b>Finding.</b> Realized-GARCH and CAViaR lead the FZO ranking; MS-GARCH fails to identify regimes with a 500-day window.

### proyectos.017
<em>Out-of-sample</em> comparison of more than 20 volatility and tail-risk models (GARCH family, HAR, CAViaR, MS-GARCH, RF-QR and LSTM) for BTC and ETH. Each model predicts one-day VaR and Expected Shortfall on a rolling 500-day window and is evaluated with coverage tests (Kupiec, Christoffersen), loss-based scores (FZO, Acerbi-Szekely) and comparison tests (Diebold-Mariano, MCS). Realized-GARCH and CAViaR lead the ranking; MS-GARCH fails to identify stable regimes with that window.

### proyectos.032
How unpredictable is a source that only looks random?

### proyectos.033
Compares randomness sources for generating cryptographic keys (BIP39/32/48/67, 2-of-3 and 3-of-5 multisig),
              separating <em>statistical quality</em> (NIST SP 800-22) from <em>real entropy</em> (min-entropy, NIST SP 800-90B).

### proyectos.034
<b>Sources.</b> CSPRNG, Mersenne Twister, RANDU, logistic map, Lorenz attractor, Rule 30, digits of π, CPU jitter, dice and card shuffles.

### proyectos.035
<b>Attacks.</b> State recovery of MT19937 in 0.02 s and of RANDU in 0.26 s, plus π-offset and clock-seed attacks.

### proyectos.036
<b>Pipeline.</b> BIP39 → BIP32 → BIP48 → BIP67, bech32 addresses (BIP173) and descriptors (BIP380).

### proyectos.038
Looking random is not the same as being unpredictable. The framework separates a source's statistical quality (NIST SP 800-22 tests) from its real entropy (min-entropy, NIST SP 800-90B) and measures how well an attacker can predict it. It recovers the MT19937 state in 0.02 s and the RANDU state in 0.26 s, and follows the impact all the way to BIP39/32/48 keys and multisig.

### proyectos.112
Does violence affect growth through institutions and investment?

### proyectos.113
Studies whether violence affects growth through institutional quality and foreign direct investment in Central America, Colombia and the Dominican Republic (2000–2024). A reproducible fixed-effects pipeline with inference robust to few clusters, plus an ML layer for triangulation.

### proyectos.115
Analyzes whether violence (homicides) deteriorates institutional quality (WGI) and, through that channel, foreign investment and growth, in 8 countries of Central America, Colombia and the Dominican Republic between 2000 and 2024. It uses two-way fixed effects with few-cluster inference (wild cluster bootstrap, CR2, Driscoll-Kraay), diagnostics and ML as triangulation. It is still under methodological review and makes no causal claim.
